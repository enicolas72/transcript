xTranscript — Open Source Acknowledgements
============================================

xTranscript embeds and links against the following open-source
components. Each component's license is reproduced (or summarised
with a pointer to the canonical text) below.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
FFmpeg (libavformat, libavcodec, libavutil, libswresample)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Source: https://ffmpeg.org/
Version shipped: see Vendor/FFmpeg-source-tag.txt in the project repo
License:  GNU Lesser General Public License v2.1 or later (LGPL-2.1+)

xTranscript links statically against an audio-only LGPL-only build of
FFmpeg. No GPL or non-free components (libx264, libx265, libfdk-aac,
libgsm, libamr, libtheora, libopencore-*, encoder_wavpack) are
compiled in or linked. The exact `./configure` flags used to produce
the build, and a SHA pinning the upstream commit, are recorded in
`Vendor/FFmpeg-source-tag.txt`.

LGPL §6 source-code availability:

  Per the LGPL, you may obtain the corresponding source code for the
  FFmpeg libraries shipped in this app — including any modifications
  applied by us (none, at the time of writing) — from:

    https://github.com/enicolas72/transcript/releases

  Each release of xTranscript that embeds an FFmpeg version attaches
  the full FFmpeg source tarball matching the SHA we built from, plus
  the `scripts/build-ffmpeg.sh` recipe.

Full LGPL v2.1 text:
  https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/lgpl-2.1.txt
